# STYLE_FORMULA_V2

Low-poly miniature toy-city rendering with soft, baked-looking materials and restrained bevels. Rounded chunky silhouettes use clean edges without harsh outlines. The environment uses warm cream sidewalks, muted asphalt, terracotta and sage storefronts; gameplay pieces and legal targets use saturated, color-safe accents. Lighting is soft daylight with gentle ambient shadows and a welcoming tabletop mood. Board components remain higher contrast than city decoration, with readable silhouettes and one consistent three-quarter top-down mobile-board perspective.
